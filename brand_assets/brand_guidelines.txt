Introduction

Purpose 

A brand guideline is a set of standards that ensures a consistent and strong brand identity across all platforms. It helps maintain clarity, professionalism, and recognition in both internal and external communication. By following these guidelines, Peers Global can create a unified presence that strengthens trust, enhances brand recall, and maximizes its impact within the business community.



About the brand 

Peers Global was created with a vision to build a powerful, community-driven platform where entrepreneurs, founders, and professionals can connect, collaborate, and grow together.

What started as an initiative to bring like-minded individuals into one network has evolved into a dynamic ecosystem focused on meaningful relationships and real business opportunities. Today, Peers Global stands as a platform that bridges industries, fosters collaboration, and enables businesses to scale through the power of connections.

Our mission

To empower entrepreneurs, founders, and professionals by building a community-driven platform that enables meaningful connections, collaboration, and real business growth.

Our vision

To build a global ecosystem where entrepreneurs and professionals connect, collaborate, and grow together creating opportunities, driving innovation, and shaping long-term business success.

Logo usage 
To maintain clarity, consistency, and visual impact, the Peers Global logo must always be used with proper spacing and size.

Always use the Peers Global logo in its original form without any modifications. Ensure strong contrast between the logo and background for maximum visibility. Maintain consistent alignment and spacing across all designs. Use the primary logo for most applications, and select appropriate variations only when space or format requires it. Avoid placing the logo on cluttered backgrounds, and ensure it remains clearly legible at all sizes.

Ensure the logo is never used below the defined minimum size to maintain clarity and readability. For print, the logo height should not be less than 25mm, and for digital use, not less than 90px. Always maintain sharp resolution and avoid pixelation. The logo should be scaled proportionally and never stretched or compressed. Consistent sizing ensures strong visibility and brand recognition across all platforms.
